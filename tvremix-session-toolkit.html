Session Scan Prompts

Run once per session. Identify PDH/PDL, structure, and bias before you do anything else. These are your level-finding prompts — output feeds the Alert Setup tab.

Workflow: Run Scan #1 first. Note the levels it returns. Then open the Alert Setup tab and fill in those exact levels before copying those prompts.
Scan S1 · Pre-Session Level Map
1 prompt
Analyze MNQ first. If MNQ is unavailable, use MCL. If I later specify another instrument, use that instead. Do the following in order: 1. State yesterday's high (PDH) and yesterday's low (PDL) with exact prices. 2. State the current session high and low so far on the active instrument. 3. Is price currently above PDH, below PDL, or between them? 4. Identify the nearest untested liquidity level above and below current price. 5. What is the current 15M and 1H structure — is it making HH/HL or LH/LL? 6. Give me a one-line directional bias for this session only. Be precise. Numbers only — no vague commentary.
Run this first. The scan output feeds any custom-level alerts you still want Remix to create.
Scan S2 · Timeframe Confluence Check
1 prompt
Still on the active instrument. Check 4 timeframes — 5M, 15M, 1H, 4H — and answer: 1. On 4H: update the key trendlines or trend direction context if needed. 2. On 1H: what is the main structure and bias? 3. On 15M: what is the most recent BOS direction? 4. On 5M: is there an entry-quality reaction forming right now? 5. Do all 4 timeframes align? Yes / Partial / No — and why. Keep it short. I need a trade / no-trade decision.
Use after S1. If TF alignment is "No", skip the session.
Scan S3 · Setup Confirmation (Pre-Entry)
1 prompt
Price is approaching [LEVEL] on [INSTRUMENT]. I'm watching for a [LONG/SHORT] setup. Check: 1. Is this level a PDH, PDL, session high/low, or FVG — confirm which. 2. Is there any reason NOT to take this trade right now? (price already extended, weak reaction, poor structure, low quality location) 3. What is the clean stop placement and what R:R does that give at a target of [TARGET]? 4. Final call: A-grade setup / B-grade setup / Skip — and one sentence why. Do not create any alerts yet. I need to confirm first.
Fill in [LEVEL], [LONG/SHORT], and [TARGET] from your S1 output before copying.

Alert Setup Prompts

After your scan gives you the levels, use these only for non-PDH/PDL custom alerts and session guards. You already have PDH/PDL arrival alerts on your charts.

Fill in levels first. The [bracketed placeholders] must be replaced with your actual prices from the session scan before you paste into Remix.
Alert A2 · Liquidity Level Proximity Alert
1–2 prompts
Create a price alert on [INSTRUMENT] for [LEVEL_PRICE]. This is a [describe: e.g. untested liquidity / session high / session low / trendline touch] level. Alert conditions: - Trigger: price crosses [LEVEL_PRICE] on a 15M bar close - Alert name: "XAU [short level name]" - Message: "XAU approaching [level name] at [LEVEL_PRICE] — review S2 TF alignment before entry" - Trigger once, then reset manually No webhook. Confirm alert created.
Use for any secondary levels identified in Scan S2.
Alert A3 · Daily Loss Limit Guard
1 prompt
Create a price alert on [INSTRUMENT] to act as a session kill-switch reminder. If my entry was at [ENTRY_PRICE] with a 1% risk stop, my account stop-out level is approximately [STOP_PRICE]. Set an alert at [STOP_PRICE]. - Name: "XAU DAILY LOSS LIMIT — STOP TRADING" - Message: "Price at your max daily risk level. Close all positions and close the platform." - Trigger: once, any timeframe This is a prop rule guard, not a trade alert. Confirm it is set.
Set this at the start of every live session. Keeps your prop rules enforced passively.

Memory Prompts — Teaching Remix Your Rules

Run these once to encode your prop rules and strategy context into the session. Remix doesn't have persistent memory between sessions, so paste the relevant rule block at the start of each session alongside your scan prompt.

How to use: Copy the Master Context block (M1) and paste it as your very first message in each Remix session. This front-loads all your rules so every subsequent response respects them without you repeating yourself.
Memory M1 · Master Context Block (Paste First Every Session)
1 prompt
Before we start: here is my trading context. Apply these rules to every response in this session. INSTRUMENTS: MNQ is primary. MCL is secondary. Other instruments may be added later and should be treated as explicitly specified only. STRATEGY: Price action only. I trade around PDH and PDL, session structure, and level reactions. TIMEFRAMES: 5M entries. 15M and 1H for checking. 4H for adjusting trendlines and broader trend context. PROP FIRM RULES (hard limits — never suggest trades that violate these): - Max risk per trade: 1% of account - Daily loss limit: $1000 — if hit, I stop trading for the day - Max drawdown: $2000 — if hit, account is at risk SESSION STATUS (fill in before pasting): - Losses today so far: [0 / 1 / 2] → If 2: flag any new setup suggestion with "REVENGE TRADE RISK — confirm you want to proceed" TRADE CRITERIA (only suggest setups that meet all of these): - Setup must be at a named level: PDH, PDL, session H/L, or identified FVG - Minimum R:R = 2:1 before I consider entry - Structure must confirm direction on 1H before entry - No revenge trading — if I have already taken two losing trades today, flag this before suggesting another PERSONALITY: Give me numbers and decisions. Only short explanations if needed.
Fill in today's loss count before pasting. 0 = fresh session. 2 = revenge-trade guard is active.
Memory M2 · Post-Trade Review Prompt
1 prompt
I just closed a trade. Help me log it quickly. Entry: [PRICE] | Exit: [PRICE] | Direction: [LONG/SHORT] Result: [WIN/LOSS] | P&L in R: [e.g. +2R or -1R] Questions: 1. Did price respect the PDH/PDL level I entered at, or did it blow through? 2. Was my stop placement logical given the current structure? 3. One thing I did correctly and one thing I could improve. 4. Updated daily P&L: I am now [+X or -X]R today. Am I within prop daily loss limit? Keep it to 4 bullet points total.
Run this within 5 minutes of closing a trade. Keeps review disciplined without eating into your quota.
Memory M3 · News Filter Check
1 prompt
Check the economic calendar for the next 2 hours. Are there any high-impact news events affecting USD or gold (XAU)? Flag: NFP, CPI, FOMC, Fed speakers, ISM, PMI, GDP, or any gold-specific events. If yes: give me the exact time and expected impact. If nothing: confirm "Clear for [X] hours." My rule: news is a context check, not an automatic ban. Flag timing and likely volatility, then let me decide.
Run this before S1 on any day you're unsure about the calendar. Saves you from getting hit by news.

Prompt Budget Guidance

Remix is capped at 15 prompts per day. Here is how to spend them across a typical trading day without burning your quota on low-value questions.

Don't waste quota on questions you already know the answer to. Remix is for level identification and alert setup. Your actual trading decision comes from your indicator and your eyes.
When Prompt Cost Priority
Session start M3 · News filter (only if unsure) 1 situational
Session start M1 · Master context block 1 required
Session start S1 · Pre-session level map 1 required
Session start S2 · TF confluence check 1 required
On level approach S3 · Setup confirmation 1 per setup
Session start or mid-session A2 · Custom level alert 1 as needed
Before any entry A3 · Daily loss guard alert 1 prop rule
After each trade M2 · Post-trade review 1 optional
Second session Repeat S1 + S2 only 2 if trading again
Non-trading / off-session I1–I5 · Investments (separate use case) 1–2 investments
Typical day breakdown
Quiet day (no trade): 3–4 prompts — M1 + S1 + S2
Normal day (1 trade): 5–7 prompts — above + S3 + A3 + M2
Active day (2 sessions): 8–11 prompts — two session scans + 2 setup checks
Investment research day: 2–4 prompts — use I1–I5 only on off-session days or after trading quota is spent wisely.
Do not exceed 15. If you hit 12, stop using Remix and trade from your existing alerts only.

Investments Prompts

For stocks and crypto long-term positions. Use these off-session or on non-trading days. These are separate from your 15-prompt trading quota — treat them as a distinct use case.

When to use this tab: Research before entering a new long-term position, reviewing existing holdings monthly, checking earnings calendars, or deciding between two candidates. These are not intraday tools — do not mix with your session scan workflow.
Invest I1 · New Position Research
1–2 prompts
Research [TICKER] for a potential long-term position. Give me the following in order: 1. Ticker overview: what the company does, sector, market cap. 2. 52-week range: current price vs. high and low. 3. Revenue growth: last 2 years YoY. Growing, flat, or declining? 4. Margins: gross and net margin — are they expanding or compressing? 5. Debt: debt-to-equity ratio. Is the balance sheet clean? 6. Cash flow: is free cash flow positive? 7. Analyst targets: consensus price target, % upside from current, and rating mix (buy/hold/sell count). 8. Weekly trend: is price above or below the 20W and 50W moving average? 9. Key catalyst: one upcoming event or driver that could move the stock. 10. Key risk: one thing that could invalidate the thesis. 11. Verdict: Buy / Watch / Avoid — one sentence. Numbers only where possible. No padding.
Replace [TICKER] with the stock or crypto symbol. Works for equities and large-cap crypto.
Invest I2 · Existing Position Review
1 prompt
I hold [TICKER]. My original thesis was: [ONE SENTENCE — e.g. "revenue acceleration + margin expansion into 2026"]. Review this position now: 1. Has the thesis changed? Is the original reason still valid? 2. What is the current price vs. my entry of [ENTRY_PRICE]? Am I up or down? 3. Are there any upcoming earnings, dividends, or key events in the next 30 days? 4. Has anything materially changed — guidance cut, management change, competitor threat? 5. Decision: Hold / Add / Trim / Exit — and one sentence why. Be direct. I do not need a full re-research — just a thesis check.
Run monthly or after any major news on a holding. Fill in ticker, thesis, and entry price.
Invest I3 · Earnings & Events Check
1 prompt
Check upcoming earnings and key events for my portfolio. My holdings are: [TICKER1, TICKER2, TICKER3 — list all]. For each: 1. Next earnings date (confirmed or estimated). 2. Any dividends or ex-dividend dates in the next 60 days. 3. Any known catalysts — product launches, regulatory decisions, analyst days. 4. Flag any that report within the next 14 days as HIGH PRIORITY. Format as a table: Ticker | Event | Date | Priority. Sort by date ascending.
Run this at the start of each month. Fill in your full holdings list before pasting.
Invest I4 · Compare Two Positions
1–2 prompts
I am deciding between [TICKER_A] and [TICKER_B] for a long-term position. Compare them on: 1. Revenue growth (last 2Y YoY) 2. Net margin 3. Analyst upside % from current price 4. Weekly trend: above or below 20W MA? 5. Relative strength: which has outperformed the S&P 500 over the last 6 months? 6. Key differentiator: one reason to prefer A over B, and one reason to prefer B over A. 7. Winner: which would you pick for a 12–18 month hold, and why in one sentence. Format as a side-by-side comparison table where possible.
Use when allocating a fixed amount between two candidates. Replace both tickers before copying.
Invest I5 · Sector Rotation Check
1 prompt
Give me a current sector rotation snapshot for US equities. 1. Top 3 strongest sectors right now — ranked by price performance and relative strength vs. SPY over the last 4 weeks. 2. Bottom 3 weakest sectors — same criteria. 3. What is the likely rotation direction over the next 1–3 months based on macro context (rates, growth, risk appetite)? 4. One sector I should be overweight and one I should be underweight given current conditions. 5. Any specific ETF or sector proxy worth watching (1 per sector max). Keep it tight. I want allocation signal, not a macro essay.
Run monthly or after major macro shifts (Fed decision, CPI print, earnings season start). No fill-ins needed.
